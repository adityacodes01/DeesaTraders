Our Story

About Deesa Traders

Strategically headquartered in New Jersey, Deesa Traders serves as a premier gateway for the global distribution of high-grade exports of Cashews, Almonds, Walnuts, Peanuts and Pistachios. Our raw and roasted nut varieties are sourced from the best growers worldwide. We offer a variety of options from raw to roasted as well as a number of bulk sizing options.

We specialize in the export of the prestigious US No.1 Supreme brand, bridging the gap between elite American harvests and the growing demands of the Indian and Middle Eastern markets. Our commitment to quality is unwavering, ensuring that only the finest produce enters the international trade stream under our banner.
